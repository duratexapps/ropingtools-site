Assumption of Risk & Disclaimer

Last updated: July 28, 2026 · Applies to: Hondo (the "Platform")

1. The nature of this activity

Team roping, and horsemanship generally, are inherently dangerous activities. They involve large, powerful animals whose behavior cannot be fully predicted or controlled, high speed, fast-moving rope and equipment, the risk of falls, being struck, kicked, dragged, or crushed, collisions, and equipment failure. Serious injury, permanent disability, and death are all real, documented possibilities in this sport — for the rider, for other participants, and for the animals involved.

By using this Platform, you acknowledge that you understand this and that no course, video, quiz, or automated feedback can eliminate these risks.

2. This is education, not supervision

This Platform provides instructional content, drills, and AI-generated feedback on video you submit. It is intended to supplement — never replace — in-person instruction from a qualified riding or roping coach who can physically observe you, your horse, your equipment, and your environment in real time.

We are not present with you when you practice or compete. We cannot see your specific situation as it develops. We cannot intervene if something goes wrong. Any decision to attempt a technique, drill, or exercise described in this Platform is made entirely by you, and entirely at your own risk.

3. The AI coaching feature specifically

The video-analysis "coach" feature uses an AI model to review still frames extracted from a video you upload and generate feedback. You should understand its real limitations:

  • It sees only a handful of still frames, not continuous video, and not the full context of your surroundings, your horse's state, or your equipment.
  • It can be wrong, incomplete, or miss something a human coach would catch immediately.
  • It has no ability to assess whether a given horse is safe for you to ride, whether your equipment is sound, or whether your physical environment is safe.
  • It is not a substitute for a live, in-person coach, and it is not veterinary or medical advice of any kind. Do not rely on it as your sole source of safety-relevant judgment.
